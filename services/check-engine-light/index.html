Check Engine Light questions, answered

Is it safe to drive with the check engine light on?

If it’s solid and the car drives normally, it’s usually safe to drive to the shop soon. If it’s flashing, that often means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter, so reduce driving and call us.

Why not just get the free code read at a parts store?

The code points to a system, not a specific failed part. We test to find the actual cause so you don’t pay for parts you didn’t need.

Will my car pass smog with the check engine light on?

No. In California, an illuminated check engine light is an automatic smog failure. We can diagnose and fix the cause before your smog check.

Do you fix electrical problems?

Yes. Batteries, alternators, starters, wiring and module faults, including the electrical gremlins European cars are known for.
