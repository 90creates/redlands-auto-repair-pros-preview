Oil Change & Maintenance questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call us at (909) 798-5153.

Do I have to go to the dealer to keep my warranty?

No. Under the federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, you can have maintenance done at an independent shop without voiding your warranty, as long as it’s done to the manufacturer’s schedule with the right parts and fluids. Keep your receipts; we document every service.

What oil does my European car need?

Most European cars require oil that carries the manufacturer’s approval, such as BMW Longlife, Mercedes-Benz 229-series or VW 502/504 specs. We look up the exact spec for your car and use oil that meets it.

How often should I change my oil?

Follow your car’s maintenance schedule or service indicator. Hot Inland Empire summers and lots of short trips count as severe driving for many manufacturers, which can mean shorter intervals.

Do you service company or rental fleets?

Yes. We’ve serviced rental fleets and can work with your business to keep vehicles on schedule. Give us a call to set it up.
