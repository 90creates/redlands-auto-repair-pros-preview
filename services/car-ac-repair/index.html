Why is my car A/C blowing warm air?

The most common causes are a refrigerant leak, a failing compressor or condenser, an electrical fault, or a stuck blend door. We diagnose which one before recommending a repair.

Can’t I just recharge it?

A recharge only helps if the system isn’t leaking. If it is, the refrigerant escapes again. We check for leaks first so you pay for a fix that lasts.

Why does my A/C smell musty?

Usually a dirty cabin air filter or moisture on the evaporator. A new cabin filter often helps, and we can clean the system if the smell persists.

Is it worth fixing the A/C on an older car?

In Redlands summers, usually yes. We’ll give you a clear quote and an honest opinion so you can decide.
