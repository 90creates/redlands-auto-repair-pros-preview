BMW Repair questions, answered

Can an independent shop work on my BMW without voiding the warranty?

Yes. Federal law protects your right to use an independent shop for maintenance and repairs, as long as the correct procedures, parts and fluids are used.

What are the most common BMW repairs?

Oil filter housing and valve cover gasket leaks, cooling system parts like water pumps and thermostats, and brake pads with wear sensors are among the most common.

What oil does my BMW need?

Most modern BMWs need oil that carries a BMW Longlife approval. We use the correct spec for your engine.

Can you reset my BMW service light?

Yes. We reset service reminders after maintenance so your car tracks its next service correctly.
