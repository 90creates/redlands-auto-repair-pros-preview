Wheel Alignment & Suspension questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call us at (909) 798-5153.

How do I know if I need an alignment?

The most common signs are the car pulling to one side, a crooked steering wheel when driving straight, and uneven tire wear. Hitting a curb or a big pothole can knock it out too.

Should I get an alignment with new tires?

It’s a good idea. A fresh alignment helps new tires wear evenly and last longer, and it’s the best time to catch worn suspension parts.

My steering wheel shakes at highway speed. Is that alignment?

Usually not. A shake at speed is more often a tire balance or bent wheel issue, while alignment causes pulling and uneven wear. We’ll check both and tell you which it is.

Can you align a lowered or lifted vehicle?

Yes. We install lowering and lift kits and align modified vehicles. Some setups need adjustable parts to reach spec, and we’ll tell you up front if yours does.
