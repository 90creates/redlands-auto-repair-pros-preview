Oil Leak & Engine Repair done right

Engine heat breaks down rubber gaskets and plastic cooling parts over time, and European engines are especially known for it. Common culprits include valve cover gaskets, oil filter housing gaskets, oil pan gaskets, water pumps and coolant hoses.

We clean and trace the leak to its source, show you what we found, and quote the repair. Catching leaks early protects belts, mounts and wiring from oil damage.

Oil Leak & Engine Repair questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call us at (909) 798-5153.

Is a small oil leak a big deal?

Not always right away, but leaks only get worse. Oil dripping onto the exhaust can smoke or catch fire, and oil-soaked belts and mounts wear out early. It’s cheaper to fix early.

Why do European cars leak oil?

Heat cycles harden rubber gaskets over time, and many European engines run hot and tight. Valve cover and oil filter housing gaskets are common on BMW and Mercedes-Benz.

What does a burning oil smell mean?

Usually oil is leaking onto hot exhaust parts. Have it checked soon; it’s often a gasket.

How much does leak repair cost?

It depends on where the leak is and how much has to come apart to reach it. We find the source first and give you a clear quote.
