Brake Repair done right

Brakes are the one system you can’t afford to guess about. When your car comes in, we measure pad thickness and rotor condition, check calipers, hoses and fluid, and tell you plainly what needs to be done now and what can wait.

Brake Repair questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call us at (909) 798-5153.

How do I know if I need new brakes?

Listen and feel. Squealing usually means pads are getting thin; grinding means metal is touching metal and you should come in right away. A soft pedal, pulling to one side, a pulsing pedal or a brake warning light are all reasons to have them checked.

Do I need new rotors, or just pads?

It depends on how much material is left and whether the rotors are warped or scored. Many European cars are designed to replace pads and rotors together. We measure them and show you before recommending anything.

How much does brake repair cost?

Price depends on your vehicle and what’s actually worn. European brake parts often cost more than domestic ones. We inspect first and give you a clear quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

Can you work on European brakes with electronic parking brakes?

Yes. Electronic parking brakes need to be put into service mode before the rear pads can be changed. We have the tools to do it correctly and reset the system afterward.

How long does a brake job take?

Many pad and rotor jobs can be finished the same day, depending on parts availability. Call ahead and we’ll tell you what to expect for your car.
