Mercedes-Benz Repair questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call us at (909) 798-5153.

Do I need to take my Mercedes to the dealer?

No. You can have your Mercedes maintained and repaired at an independent shop without affecting your warranty, as long as factory schedules and correct parts and fluids are used.

What are Service A and Service B?

They’re Mercedes-Benz’s alternating maintenance visits. Service A is a smaller service built around an oil change and inspection; Service B adds more items like a cabin filter and brake fluid. Your dash tells you which one is due.

What Mercedes models do you work on?

Sedans, coupes and SUVs. Give us a call with your model and the issue, and we’ll let you know how we can help.

What are common Mercedes repairs?

Oil leaks from aging gaskets, brake pads and sensors, suspension wear and electrical faults are some of the most common. We’ll tell you what’s urgent and what can wait.
