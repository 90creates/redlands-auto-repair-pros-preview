Frequently asked questions

Do I have to go to the dealer for maintenance to keep my warranty?

No. Federal law lets you use an independent shop for maintenance and repairs without voiding your warranty, as long as the work follows the manufacturer’s schedule with the correct parts and fluids. We document every service for your records.

What kinds of cars do you work on?

We specialize in foreign cars, both European and Asian imports, including BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, VW, Porsche, Toyota, Lexus, Honda and more. We work on domestic vehicles too.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We inspect your car, explain what we found, and give you a clear quote. Nothing gets done without your OK.
